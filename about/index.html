About Memories: Save to Photos

Memories: Save to Photos is a small iOS app with one job: take the official export of your Snapchat Memories and turn it back into a proper part of your photo library, with the real dates, places and captions restored. It exists because that export, on its own, arrives as a heap of scrambled files that no photo app knows how to read.

What the app is

It reads the zip files from Snapchat's My Data export, matches every photo and video to its record in the export, and writes each memory into your iPhone's photo library with its true capture date, time and location, and its captions and drawings composited back on. The result is that your memories sort into the right years, appear on the map, and sit alongside your camera photos, exactly as if they had been taken with your phone.

How it works with your data

Everything happens on your device. The app has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your export files and photos never leave your iPhone, and the app works the same with the internet turned off. There is a fuller account in the privacy policy, but that is the whole idea: your memories are yours, not anyone's data.

Independent, and honest about it

This is an independent app, made by one developer rather than a company. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Snap Inc., and Snapchat is a trademark of Snap Inc. The app never touches your Snapchat account; it only ever reads a file you downloaded yourself. There are no fake reviews and no invented statistics anywhere on this site, because a tool that handles your memories should be trustworthy on the small things too.

Pricing

The first 100 memories are free, on your real export, so you can see the result before deciding. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks everything. There is no subscription and no recurring charge; once your memories are saved to your library, they are yours and the app never has to be opened again.

Common questions

Is Memories: Save to Photos an official Snapchat app?

No. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Snap Inc. It works only with a data export you download yourself through Snapchat’s official My Data page, and it never connects to Snapchat or accesses your account. Snapchat is a trademark of Snap Inc.

Who makes Memories: Save to Photos?

It is made by an independent developer, not a large company. There is no marketing team and no investors behind it, just a tool built to solve one specific problem well. If you email the support address, you reach the person who made the app.

Is it safe to use with my photos?

The app runs entirely on your device. It has no servers, no account and no analytics, so your export files and photos never leave your iPhone, and it works the same with the internet turned off. It saves memories to your library and only removes a photo if you undo an import yourself.
