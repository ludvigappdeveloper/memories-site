A common export problem

Why your Snapchat export has the wrong dates, and how to fix it

You exported years of Snapchat Memories, opened them, and every single one is dated today. Nothing is broken and nothing is lost. Here is exactly why it happens, and how to get the real dates back.

This is the single most common surprise with a Snapchat export, and the good news is that the real dates were never deleted. They are just stored in a place your photo app does not look by default. Once you understand where they live, restoring them is straightforward, by hand or automatically.

Why do all my exported Snapchat photos have the same date?

Because the date you see is the day you exported them, not the day they were taken. Snapchat stores each memory's real capture time in its own database, not inside the image file. When it builds your export, the files are written fresh that day, so every one carries the export date and they all pile up together in your library.

Why is the date on my exported memories today's date?

Today's date is the export date. The photo and video files in a Snapchat export have no original capture date baked in, so your phone falls back to the file's creation date, which is the moment Snapchat generated the export. The real dates are not lost; they are stored separately in a file called memories_history.json.

Can I fix the dates after exporting?

Yes, as long as your export includes the JSON files. The file memories_history.json lists every memory with its true capture date, time and, where known, location. Anything that reads that file can write the correct date back into each photo and video, either by hand with a free tool or automatically with an app.

How do I restore the real capture dates?

Two ways. By hand: unzip the export on a computer, read memories_history.json, and use a free metadata tool such as ExifTool to write each date into the file's DateTimeOriginal field. Automatically: open the export in Memories: Save to Photos, which matches every file to its JSON entry and writes the real date, time and location on your iPhone. The first 100 memories are free.

The manual recipe is written out step by step in the export guide. It works well on a computer for a small library, and gets tedious at scale, which is the reason the app exists.

Do I need to export again to fix the dates?

Only if your export has no JSON files. If you used the Request Only Memories shortcut, the JSON was left out and there is nothing to restore the dates from, so request the export again with Export JSON Files switched on. If you already have the JSON files, you do not need to re-export; you just process what you have.

Not sure how to request it properly? See how to download your Snapchat data for the exact switches.

Does fixing the dates also restore locations?

It can, because the same memories_history.json file also records where each memory was captured, when Snapchat had that information. Writing the location into the photo's metadata puts the memory back on your library's map, just like a photo taken with the built-in camera. The app restores dates and locations in the same pass.
